1. Executive Summary & Vision (Project Concept)

OBEX (Aerospace Exoplanet Discovery Suite) is a research-grade astronomical computing platform designed to bridge the gap between raw ground-based telescope telemetry and peer-reviewed astrophysical models published by NASA, Caltech, and international astronomical observatories.

The system ingests and processes differential photometric light curves, calculates transit depth dips ($\Delta F$), performs multi-epoch phase folding, models CCD thermal dark noise, systematically eliminates 11 distinct false-positive failure modes, simulates relativistic interstellar starflight trajectories, and generates telemetry-grounded AI science narratives via Gemini 2.5 Flash.

3. Autonomous AI Science Grounding (Gemini 2.5 Flash)

The platform integrates an autonomous telemetry-grounded explainer powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash. Real-time light curve geometry, ingress/egress boundaries, and host stellar parameters are fed directly into the model to deliver:

  • Bilingual Scientific Narratives: Arabic & English tailored for three distinct audiences (Students, Professional Astrophysicists, and the Public).
  • Physical Derivation Synthesis: Transit depth interpretation, planet-to-star radius ratio ($R_p / R_*$), and atmospheric constraint evaluation.
  • Observational Boundaries & Caveats: Honest appraisal of ground-based atmospheric seeing and scintillation limits.

5. Relativistic Starflight Mechanics & Habitability Engine

🪐 Kopparapu (2013) Habitable Zone

Calculates 4th-order polynomial flux thresholds and spatial distance boundaries in AU:

d = √(L* / Seff)
Teq = Teff × √(R* / 2a) × (1 - A)^(1/4)

Distinguishes Runaway Greenhouse (inner limit) from Maximum CO2 Condensation (outer limit).

🚀 Special Relativity Starflight Mechanics

Calculates time dilation and coordinate flight times at relativistic fractions of light ($\beta = v/c$):

γ = 1 / √(1 - β²)
τ = t_earth / γ | Δt = t_earth - τ

Quantifies biological time dilation savings for interstellar missions to target exoplanets.
